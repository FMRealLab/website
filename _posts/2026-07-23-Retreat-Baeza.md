We recently gathered in Baeza for our scientific retreat along with Lupiañez Lab and Acemel Lab, hosted at the beautiful Sede Antonio Machado (UNIA). Over several days, we shared updates on our ongoing projects, discussed recent findings, and explored new ideas that will help shape our future experiments and research directions.

Great science starts with great conversations, and this retreat was full of them. Thanks to everyone for the engaging discussions and inspiring atmosphere!
