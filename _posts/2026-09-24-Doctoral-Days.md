The Universidad Pablo de Olavide (UPO) and Instituto de la Grasa (CSIC) along with the Organizer Comitte of the Doctoral Programme in Biotecnology, Engineering, and Chemical Technology organized the XII edition of the Doctoral Days. This event provides an opportunity for students from different disciplines to come
together over two days and share their experiences. 

In this edition Nacho and Rubén brought a poster to the poster session and Rubén was one of the PhD student selected to give a talk about his project.
