Michael is a fascinating scientist working at the interface of comparative genomics🧬, bioinformatics🖥️, and molecular biology🥼. His research uses cross-species approaches to uncover fundamental principles of genome regulation and understand how these mechanisms shape development and human disease.

**Title of the seminar**: Using comparative and functional genomics to understand cardiovascular development and disease gene regulation.
